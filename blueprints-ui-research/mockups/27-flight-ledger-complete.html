    Starting WordPress…

    The consolidation pass: Design 26’s single aligned sheet keeps Paper Blueprint’s headline-level Add actions, while installed themes and plugins open directly when clicked. Popovers anchor to their trigger, never cover it, and scroll internally under a pinned search; one surface holds installed items and WordPress.org discovery, URLs, uploads and drops. Finishing steps are grouped — recipes, developer tools, then write-your-own PHP, SQL, or WP-CLI in a proper editor — and credited once to Alex Kirk’s step library. Runtime facts live in Boot; content defaults live with content; pages edit in an embedded WordPress Playground; broad undo and deliberate focus restoration throughout. JSON and Run use one valid Blueprint; uploads launch in an embedded preview so their bytes travel with it.
