DESIGN 5 Notebook Blueprint — prose + steps + code, interleaved. For teaching & sharing. about this ▲

A Blueprint as a runnable document (Observable / Jupyter). Cells interleave plain-language step capsules, prose that explains the “why”, and first-class raw-code cells. Each semantic cell has a “show code” toggle — the cleanest realization of layered disclosure: friendly by default, exact Blueprint step one tap away. The escape hatch (runPHP) isn't buried in an “advanced” drawer; it's a legible code cell sitting right next to the sentence that motivates it.

This is the format's super-power for the ecosystem: Blueprints double as tutorials, docs, and demos (contributor-day recipes, “here's how to reproduce my bug”, plugin READMEs). It leans into Adam's “Playground Blueprints of today → WordPress Recipes of tomorrow.” Weakness: less efficient for editing a big config than the list/inspector — best for blueprints meant to be read, not just run.
