Everything above is one blueprint.json — press Run to build a fresh site from it. Nothing is installed until then.

The bet: zero navigation — the whole Blueprint is one non-scrolling poster on a fixed grid of zones, and every fact has a permanent home. Nothing ever moves, expands, or reflows; every edit happens in a small popover anchored to the fact you clicked, so spatial memory does all the wayfinding. After one visit you stop searching: versions live under the title, plugins in the middle, the theme is always the big picture on the left. The map is the interface — if it never changes, reading it costs nothing.
