DESIGN 4 AI Conversational Builder — describe it, get editable step cards. about this ▲

The most direct expression of “speak the user's language.” Realizes the AI-first thread (Matías Ventura: “build with AI in mind from day one”; Artur Piszek's GPT prototypes; Alex Kirk's “Generate AI instructions”; Playground's PLAYGRD-610 Blueprint tool). Describe an outcome → the assistant emits a real Blueprint that materializes as editable cards on the right. The chat is a generator, not the source of truth — the cards/JSON are, so you never lose the ability to hand-edit.

Anti-black-box guardrails: every generated step has an “explain” affordance that reveals the exact underlying Blueprint step (the bridge that lets a curious non-dev graduate to a dev), a “What's missing?” completeness critic, and one-click escape to Cards or raw JSON. Matías's other point — using the AI to discover what Blueprint syntax can't yet express — falls out naturally.

Pattern: Plain-language → primitivesPattern: Explain / detail-on-demandPattern: Escape to cards/JSONAI-first (M. Ventura, A. Piszek)Backs: Alex Kirk “AI instructions” · PLAYGRD-610

Caveat to weigh: best as an entry mode layered over concepts 1–2, not a replacement — an AI that emits an unrunnable Blueprint is worse than a blank card. Pair generation with the same lenient live-validation.
