DESIGN 1 Recipe Cards — plain-language, inline-editable steps. Friendly-first, correct-by-construction. about this ▲

A Blueprint is an ordered list of typed steps — the exact mental model of Gutenberg blocks, which this whole audience already knows. Realizes Adam's stated vision: “the step library makes perfect sense in Gutenberg — custom steps would be just custom blocks, predefined sites with customizable parts are just partially-synced patterns.” Editing happens inline in the card (not a detached inspector) per his note that “moving inputs away from the canvas is friction.”

Progressive disclosure layered here: collapsed card = plain-language outcome → expand for Basic fields → Advanced reveal for power knobs (2 levels max, per NN/g) → a Run PHP escape hatch card lives in the same list so devs are never blocked. Status dots mark cards carrying non-default/advanced settings so nothing important hides silently (Webflow idiom). The Simple / Advanced mode switch (top-right) hides or reveals the code-heavy surface (Francesco Bigiarini's “simple mode / advanced mode”).
