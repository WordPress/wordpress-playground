    The bet: the page is the literal answer to “what happens when I press Run”, so trust anxiety
    has nowhere to live. 6 numbered stages read as one ordered story — boot, theme, plugins,
    content, users, finish — each a future-tense outcome with its controls sitting inside the row.
    Nothing hides in tabs or accordions; the only expandable list scrolls inside itself, so the
    6-stage map never leaves the screen. The timeline terminates in the single blue element on
    the page: the Run button, placed like a checkout confirm.
